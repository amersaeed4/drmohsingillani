It is a group of metabolic diseases characterized by increased levels of glucose in the blood ( hyperglycemia ), resulting from defects in either or both of the following :

**1) Insulin production:** Pancreatic Beta cells may stop secreting insulin i.e (Type-1 Diabetes Mellitus).

**2) Insulin Action:** The body cells may stop responding to insulin or there is insulin resistance e (Type-2 Diabetes Mellitus)

## Types Of Diabetes Mellitus

**Type-1**

In diabetes type 1, the pancreas does not make insulin, because the body's immune system attacks the islet cells in the pancreas that make insulin.

- Genetic Predisposition – HLA genes

- Autoimmune disease against Beta cells in the pancreas . Possibly viral, which causes destruction of Beta cells ( Decreased Insulin Production )

**Type-2**

Insulin ( high, normal or low) present but can not be utilized by body cells i.e ( Insulin resistance ) >> Other specific types ( Secondary)

- Gestational Diabetes : Typically associated with pregnant women

- LADA (Latent autoimmune diabetes in Adults )
